# 29 — Close the billing, allowance, guarantee and Post-Term lifecycle

**What to build:** Complete the commercial lifecycle after initial purchase: usage/allowance visibility, operation preview and reservation, add-on capacity, product-failure restoration, Billing Recovery, guarantee/refund eligibility, cancellation, paid-term end, and 30-day Post-Term read-only export. Commercial state must never silently override source, judgment, readiness, or Recipient authority.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Turn a qualified buyer into a reconciled Individual entitlement; 27 — Recover Account security and explicitly resume Recipient Access; 28 — Pause, archive and reactivate a Deal without losing authority.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Usage surfaces and 70/90-percent warnings distinguish Active Deal slots, pages/files, full-workflow operations, intensive processing, archive capacity, and unmetered actions under exact term rules.
- [ ] An expensive operation exposes preview, consent, reservation, completion/failed effect, provider/page/cost identity, and release or consumption exactly once.
- [ ] Product-side failure restores consumed allowance once and retains guarantee eligibility when the confirmed conditions remain satisfied.
- [ ] Guarantee fixtures approve an incorrectly accepted supported path that later fails product-side, and reject post-first-value, repeated account/payment-instrument, unsupported source/authority, or otherwise ineligible requests.
- [ ] Add-on purchase requires explicit preview/consent, grants only the exact future capacity, and cannot apply retroactively.
- [ ] Cancellation preserves the paid term, then disables new work and starts 30-day Post-Term read-only/export; Post-Term revokes Recipient Access under the permanent contract.
- [ ] Billing Recovery, refund effects, dispute outcomes, duplicate/reordered provider evidence, capacity, receipt, entitlement, and Audit all reconcile idempotently.
- [ ] Black-box commercial mutations satisfy AC-015 through AC-018 and AC-069.

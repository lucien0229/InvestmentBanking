# Design Premium Self-Serve Monetization and Unit Economics

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02, 03, 04, 06, 08

## Question

What value metric, billing unit, packaging, individual and future team tiers, price hypothesis, usage controls, refund or preview mechanism, direct-cost envelope, and expansion path let the product command a premium price through Self-Serve Purchase?

Ground the design in the selected workflow's outcome, repeat cadence, source costs, model and document-processing costs, support burden, and current professional software pricing evidence. Preserve a complete individual product rather than a crippled lead-in tier. Team packaging may add organizational value later but cannot be required for the Individual-First Release. Produce a linked Markdown decision asset with assumptions and sensitivity ranges; do not treat low competitor prices as a reason to lower the product's intended value.

## Answer

Sell one complete **Individual Deal Desk** by Active Deal Workspace capacity: **$995/month or $10,950/year paid upfront**, including two concurrent Active Deals and every core Controlled Auction Execution Package capability. Additional complete Active Deal capacity is **$500/month or $5,500/year**. Evidence inspection, citations, corrections, deterministic recalculation, QC, review, Human Decisions, normal targeted Revisions, exports, and product-failure recovery are deliberately not metered.

Each Active Deal includes a disclosed operating envelope of 250 newly processed files, 2,500 logical pages, 25 GB active storage, and 20 defined full-workflow operations per billing month. A genuinely exceptional Deal can purchase a visible, consent-gated **$1,000 Large Source Packet & Intensive Processing Pack**; no black-box AI credits or surprise overage apply. Public synthetic outcome proof precedes checkout, real confidential uploads begin only after payment, and a conditional 14-day First-Deal Control-Loop Guarantee refunds the first charge when supported, authorized inputs pass preflight but product-side failure prevents the defined first value.

The low/base/high model is fully recomputable. At the selected monthly price, the base case estimates **$217.62 cash direct cost, 78.1% cash gross margin, $292.62 contribution cost including support, and 70.6% contribution margin**. Launch cash/contribution ranges are 70%–80% / 65%–75%; steady-state objectives are at least 85% / 75%. The unguarded high case is economically broken, so exceptionally large processing is disclosed before execution while normal evidence and control work stays unmetered. Future Team packaging adds shared Deals, seats, billing, templates, collaboration, roles, approvals, organization audit, policy and administration; it does not gate Individual core capability.

# Constrain AI to versioned proposal-only tasks

V1 executes AI only through immutable, versioned AI Task Definitions that bind permitted input classes, minimal selected context, product and domain instructions, strict output contracts, Evidence policy, provider capability, resource limits, and evaluation gates. Source content is presented as untrusted data; the model receives no Deal-wide authority, browser, network, storage, code-execution, or autonomous tool access, and the product does not implement a general agent that may expand its own scope. AI output remains an AI Proposal or Evidence Candidate and cannot directly create a Fact, Human Decision, Process Event, external-use authorization, or business side effect; schema, domain, authorization, Native Locator, and deterministic checks must succeed before a controlled worker may commit any accepted result idempotently.

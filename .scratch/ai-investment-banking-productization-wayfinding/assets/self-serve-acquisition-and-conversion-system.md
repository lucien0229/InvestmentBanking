# Self-Serve Acquisition and Conversion System

Status: Product design decision for Ticket 11  
Decision date: 2026-08-01  
Launch market: United States, English, USD  
Research companion: [Ticket 11 Authoritative Acquisition Source Notes](ticket-11-self-serve-acquisition-authoritative-source-notes.md)

## Decision in one page

The recommended system is one closed, inspectable path:

`trigger-specific discovery → synthetic controlled-outcome proof → pricing and qualification preview → card checkout → paid authority/confidentiality preflight → guided first control loop → persistent Deal Execution Desk → material Revision and Package Readiness → referral and future Team signal`

It does not depend on founder sales, mandatory demos, direct outreach, interviews, advisers, offline implementation, enterprise procurement, custom onboarding, or high-touch support.

| Decision | Recommended contract |
|---|---|
| Category | **Controlled sell-side auction execution workspace**; “AI for investment banking” is only a descriptive secondary phrase |
| Target | Execution-oriented Individual Banker with purchase authority and separate Deal-data-use authority at a boutique investment bank, small/midsize M&A advisory firm, or independent transaction advisory practice |
| Premium job | Keep an authorized Sell-Side Auction source-to-model-to-native-package chain current, tied out, reviewable, and explicitly controlled through every material Revision |
| Hero outcome | A continuously refreshable **Controlled Auction Execution Package** inside a persistent Deal Workspace |
| Primary public proof | A synthetic Deal Workspace in which the user inspects exact lineage, corrects the `$6.2m` Cash extraction to `$4.7m`, scopes the `$18.4m` versus `$17.8m` EBITDA conflict, observes the `$1.5m` deterministic tie-out recovery, and sees the affected artifacts, QC, Package Readiness, and authorization consequence |
| Primary CTA | **Inspect the synthetic Deal Workspace**; price-aware visitors may go directly to **See pricing and qualification** |
| Commercial path | `$995/month` or `$10,950/year paid upfront`; two Active Deal Workspaces; direct card checkout; no free confidential-material trial |
| Activation | The paid guided path closes one real evidence → typed Banker decision → deterministic validation → affected native/reader output loop; it never invents a conflict to manufacture value |
| Retention | New Source Records, material impacts, immutable Revisions, readiness, milestones, exports, archive/reactivation, and new Deals—not repeat chat sessions |
| Initial acquisition order | **1. Trigger Search-to-Control Proof; 2. Artifact Utility-to-Workspace; 3. Recorded Control-Loop Proof Distribution** |
| Trust rule | Every market-facing claim must link to an observable product mechanism, exact qualification, and implemented/verified control; an unimplemented security promise is not launch copy |
| Expansion | Referral and artifact loops begin only after controlled value; future Team adds organization value and never unlocks Individual core capability |

The first three loops share the same proof, qualification, checkout, activation, and measurement contracts. They are not three unrelated campaigns.

## 0. Epistemic and scope contract

### Labels

- **Verified fact / verified mechanism** — observed on a current primary or first-party source, or fixed by an approved Ticket 1–10 contract. A competitor surface proves only that the provider exposes that surface.
- **Evidence-backed inference** — a bounded implication drawn from verified facts, not a measured result for this product.
- **Product design decision** — the behavior this product will implement and test.
- **Channel hypothesis** — a discoverability, message, traffic, or conversion proposition without this product's live cohort evidence.
- **Unresolved validation question** — something production traffic, purchase, activation, retention, cost, or support telemetry must answer; it is not a build/no-build gate.

### Non-negotiable inherited boundaries

This decision inherits Tickets 2–10 without reopening them: solo, low-touch operation; execution-owner ICP; Sell-Side Auction; one authoritative Deal Workspace; immutable Source Records, Evidence, Claims, Facts, Assumptions, Human Decisions and Revisions; Ticket 6 authority/confidentiality/security controls; evidence-bounded AI; deterministic closure; exact native and reader artifacts; separate readiness and exact-Revision external-use authorization; the validated `C discovery → A first correct use → B persistent desk` product form; complete Individual capability; and Ticket 10's exact price, capacity, guarantee and repricing rules.

It does not create or publish a page, article, template, video, ad, analytics stack, email campaign, referral program, or production code. It does not make a legal, privacy, tax, advertising, regulated-opinion, valuation, transaction-completion, or professional-correctness claim.

### Claim discipline

Use mechanism-bound verbs: **trace, inspect, flag, calculate, tie out, preserve, refresh, regenerate affected scope, record a decision, block, export**. Do not use: **10x, zero error, fully autonomous, complete the entire deal, guarantee accuracy, eliminate risk, improve valuation, guarantee close, replace the banker**. Quantified time savings remain prohibited until instrumented real cohorts support an exact, qualified statement.

## 3. Channel Research and Prioritization

### Prioritized initial acquisition loops

#### 1. Trigger Search-to-Control Proof — launch first

High-intent organic search and a bounded exact-match paid-search experiment land on a trigger-specific workflow/problem page. The page opens the relevant state inside Project Northstar rather than a generic feature tour. The visitor completes the public control loop, sees price/qualification, and checks out. This loop has the clearest intent and the least dependence on personality-led distribution.

**Hypothesis:** a Banker already experiencing a source, tie-out, refresh, CIM/model, readiness, or Deal-control problem will prefer mechanism proof over an “AI for bankers” promise.  
**Metric chain:** behaviorally qualified visit → proof start/completion → pricing view → checkout start/completion → preflight → first value.

#### 2. Artifact Utility-to-Workspace — launch second

Rights-cleared synthetic Source Packet, package manifest, editable sample artifacts, readiness/source-inventory checklists, and a bounded tie-out/readiness calculator solve a concrete preparatory problem. Every object links to its exact state in the interactive workspace and then to pricing. No email gate is required for essential proof or downloads.

**Hypothesis:** a useful professional artifact earns attention and demonstrates the product's control model better than a feature list.  
**Metric chain:** artifact view/download → lineage deep-link → proof completion → pricing → purchase.

#### 3. Recorded Control-Loop Proof Distribution — launch third

One canonical recorded walkthrough of the conflict, extraction correction, deterministic recovery, downstream impact and return Revision is segmented into professional educational pieces and distributed through owned search pages, LinkedIn, newsletters/podcasts/creators, and rule-compliant communities. Distribution always deep-links into the canonical interactive proof; it does not require a founder demo or direct message.

**Hypothesis:** a visual, exact control loop can make a complex premium mechanism intelligible to audiences not yet expressing a direct search query.  
**Metric chain:** qualified play → control-loop chapter completion → proof deep-link → pricing → purchase.

## 4. Search and Content Architecture

### Architecture rule

One page should own one material trigger, job, deliverable, proof, or trust question and deep-link to the matching Project Northstar state. Pages may share the same proof objects, but may not multiply into city, industry, job-title, file-type, or competitor-name variants without genuinely distinct intent and evidence. No page is published merely because a keyword can be generated.

Third-party trademarks may appear only in accurate, fair comparison or compatibility context. Titles, URLs, ads and metadata must not imply affiliation, official status, or a missing competitor feature that has not been verified. No competitor's name becomes the core value proposition.

### Canonical proof sequence

1. Show the complete package and its independent readiness states before asking for files.
2. Let the visitor open exact synthetic Source Records and native locators.
3. Preserve the two EBITDA values as a conflict; never silently blend them.

## 13. Funnel Metrics and Event Contract

### Common data and cohort rules

- Analytics stores pseudonymous session, account, Deal, Revision and object identifiers; event name, timestamp, product version, trigger category, state/step, duration, counts and reason codes. It does **not** store client/Deal/buyer names, source text, filenames, extracted values, formulas, bids, contact lists, artifact excerpts, Human Decision text or confidential notification payloads.
- Billing identity/payment data remains in the billing system; product analytics receives only plan/term/amount category, success/failure and processor-safe reason category.
- Exclude bots/crawlers, employees/test accounts, automated monitoring, duplicate retry emissions, synthetic E2E tests, refunded fraud/chargebacks and known instrumentation failures as stated per metric. Public synthetic proof events remain explicitly `synthetic=true` and never count as paid activation.
- Report cohorts by acquisition loop/channel/referrer, first landing trigger, query/ad cell where applicable, proof version, price shown, monthly/annual, packet/source/template class, product version, real conflict/correction presence, first-value outcome, guarantee/refund and lifecycle maturity.

### Funnel ratios and reading order

Read conversion in causal order, never as one blended “site conversion” number:

1. `qualified_visit / human landing session` — traffic and targeting;
2. `proof start / qualified visit` — message and proof relevance;
3. `proof completion / proof start` — proof comprehension/mechanism;
4. `pricing view / proof completion` — proof-to-commercial intent;
5. `checkout start / qualified pricing view` — price/trust/qualification understanding;
6. `clean checkout completion / checkout start` — payment/checkout and displayed-offer acceptance;
7. `paid preflight completion / clean purchase` — authority/security/compatibility;
8. `first value / eligible minimum packet` and `/ clean purchase` — product/activation;
9. `first export / first value`, `retained lifecycle point / matured first value`, `renewal / at-risk subscription` — persistent value;
10. `referral or Team signal / eligible matured account` — advocacy/organization expansion.

No industry benchmark is used. Any later numeric target must be labeled **Product Design Decision** or **initial instrumentation target**, state its denominator/cohort/window and remain separate from observed results. The only inherited numeric learning floor is Ticket 10's repricing rule: before list-price change, seek at least **50 clean paid Individual purchases, 25 first-value completions and three mature monthly usage cohorts**; this is not statistical proof or a product no-go gate.

### Failure taxonomy and response

| Failure class | Observable signature | Recommended next action |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic problem | Too few behaviorally qualified visits; irrelevant queries/referrers dominate | Narrow/replace trigger, query, distribution or source; do not change product price/proof from impression data |
| Message problem | Qualified visitor sees page but rarely starts relevant proof; exits indicate category/ICP confusion | Tighten trigger→outcome→boundary and deep-link to exact proof state |
| Proof problem | Proof starts but users cannot complete, inspect mechanism, or connect it to their job | Repair interaction, sequence, artifacts, accessible walkthrough or mechanism explanation |
| Trust problem | Proof/pricing interest exists but security, data use, accuracy, continuity or employer-policy surfaces block progression | Supply implemented evidence, narrow claims/use or block launch scope; never borrow competitor controls |
| Price/packaging problem | Qualified proof completers understand trust/fit, start pricing evaluation, but consistently reject the exact commercial object/term while checkout works | Preserve reason-coded cohort; test term/presentation only under Ticket 10 rules; do not confuse with message/proof/reliability |
| Checkout problem | Intent reaches payment but form, card, tax, invoice, auth or entitlement fails | Repair checkout/retry/receipt/entitlement; do not add sales fallback |
| Preflight compatibility problem | Clean buyers pay but authority, input, template, security or minimum packet fails | Move truth earlier, improve compatibility/remediation, or narrow eligible route; refund when product wrongly accepted input |
| Product reliability problem | Eligible packet reaches work but parse/model/check/render/export fails or retries | Restore capacity, fix root cause, apply guarantee; do not outsource completion to founder |
| Activation problem | Product functions but user cannot choose scope, inspect Evidence, decide, close checks or see downstream value | Simplify/clarify guided first correct use and resumable recovery; preserve necessary professional friction |
| Retention problem | First value/export occurs but no material return loop, active Deal continuation, new Deal or renewal | Diagnose Deal cadence, state preservation, event detection, notification fatigue and ongoing outcome—not “send more engagement emails” |

Experiment failure changes the next mechanism tested. It never becomes a no-build gate and does not authorize Ticket 11 to alter Ticket 10's price/package in disguise.

## 15. First-10 and First-50 Self-Serve Buyer Plan

### Recommended first channel

Start with **Trigger Search-to-Control Proof**, specifically the three most concrete problem clusters:

1. model/CIM/reader inconsistency and tie-out before review;
2. new actuals or Source Record requiring a controlled refresh/Revision; and
3. Sell-Side Auction source/package readiness.

They have exact demonstrable failure/recovery states, lead naturally to Ticket 9's first-value proof, and do not require a prospect to understand a new broad category first. This is a Channel hypothesis, not verified demand or a traffic forecast.

### Launch asset set required before counting clean buyers

1. category/outcome page and three trigger pages;
2. no-signup Project Northstar interactive Deal Workspace;
3. public synthetic Source Packet and annotated native locators;
4. downloadable editable workbook/native material samples, exact reader copies, evidence/control records and sample export manifest;
5. canonical recorded walkthrough with captions, transcript and deep-linked chapters;
6. methodology page for evidence, AI/deterministic/Banker control, QC, readiness and authorization;
7. implemented-fact-only security/confidentiality/data-use/training/retention/deletion page;
8. source/template/authority/intended-use qualification preview;
9. exact pricing, capacity, cancellation, billing/invoice and conditional-guarantee pages;
10. account, checkout, paid preflight, guided activation, export and self-serve recovery paths;
11. the event/identity/privacy contract and a funnel reason-code view; and
12. one source-inventory checklist and one model/CIM tie-out/readiness utility, each mapped to the relevant proof state.

This is an asset contract for later production, not content production in Ticket 11.

### Community, paid and referral boundaries

- Community participation, if later used, is rule-compliant public education or a rights-cleared synthetic utility; no cold DMs, promotion where forbidden, invented user stories, anonymous astroturfing, confidential examples or claims that discussion proves demand. Social discussion remains anecdotal/self-selected.
- The first paid-search cell uses only one trigger cluster, exact/phrase match, explicit negative keywords for jobs, careers, salaries, free courses, interview prep, generic ChatGPT prompts and consumer investing, and the Phase 1 `$1,500` total ceiling. No competitor-trademark deception, broad “investment banking” targeting or retargeting at launch.
- Referral is not needed to obtain the first buyer. It appears only after a user reaches first value; reward follows clean full-price referred purchase, first value and guarantee-window exit. No Deal artifact/contact upload is required.

### What counts as clean paid-buyer evidence

A **clean paid Individual purchase** is a unique non-test, non-fraud account that:

- pays the displayed `$995` monthly or `$10,950` annual Individual price through the self-serve checkout;
- receives no buyer discount, free term, complimentary seat or founder-negotiated term;
- has no mandatory call, demo, direct outreach, manual implementation or founder-completed Deal work;
- has attested purchase authority and the in-scope intended-use boundary;
- is not a duplicate charge, chargeback, guarantee refund or other refund; and
- has an attributable discovery/proof/checkout path or an explicit direct/unknown classification.

Report **clean eligible purchases** separately: clean paid purchases that also pass paid authority/confidentiality/input preflight. Report first value, export and retention separately rather than silently requiring them to call a payment “clean.” Referral buyers may remain clean only when they pay full price; the referrer's later account credit is recorded as channel cost.

### First 10 plan

| Order | Action | Evidence sought | Decision after evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Release P0 proof/trust/pricing/checkout instrumentation, then the three trigger pages | Real qualified visits and event integrity | Repair broken message/proof/trust/funnel before buying more traffic |
| 2 | Expose utilities and recorded chapters from the same pages; distribute through owned search surfaces and bounded LinkedIn/newsletter/community tests | Which entry state yields proof completion and pricing views | Prioritize the winning entry state; retain canonical proof |
| 3 | Run the one-cell paid-search experiment within Phase 1 ceiling | Real query/referrer quality and proof behavior, not clicks alone | Add negatives/narrow trigger/stop cell; no broad-match expansion |
| 4 | Allow direct full-price checkout and paid preflight; do not intervene in individual sessions | First clean purchases, exact abandonment/preflight reason codes | Fix checkout/compatibility/reliability at its layer |
| 5 | Observe activation, first export, guarantee/refund and material return | Whether clean eligible buyers reach controlled value without human onboarding | Repair guide/recovery/product reliability; do not convert support into service |
| 6 | Enable V1 referral only after first-value users exist | Safe share, clean full-price referral, abuse and credit cost | Keep, cap, change or pause referral independently of product access |

The objective is to accumulate the first 10 clean purchases with a legible path and downstream status—not to guarantee a buyer count or a date. Ten purchases do not validate price, channel repeatability or retention.

### Path from 10 to 50 clean Individual purchases

1. Freeze the definition of clean purchase and preserve every price/proof/channel cohort.
2. Rank triggers by `qualified visit → proof completion → clean purchase → eligible preflight → first value`, not by sessions or clicks.
3. Expand only the strongest trigger into its adjacent workflow, deliverable and methodology pages; reject low-quality programmatic multiplication.
4. Deepen Loop 2 only where utility users proceed to mechanism proof; stop utilities that attract template-only traffic without qualified product evaluation.
5. Segment the canonical recording only when chapter/deep-link data shows a comprehension gap; do not create a video volume treadmill.
6. Scale one paid query/partner/distribution cell at a time after clean downstream evidence; calculate spend against observed contribution/refund/retention, not an industry CAC benchmark.
7. Add authentic customer proof only with exact authorization and evidence. Until then, Project Northstar remains the public proof; no invented testimonials or implied logos.
8. Track monthly versus annual selection, second material Revision, new Deal, renewal/cancel and referral separately; do not count annual prepayment as proof of realized retention.
9. Preserve Ticket 10's repricing learning floor: seek **50 clean paid purchases, 25 first-value completions and three mature monthly usage cohorts** before a list-price change. This is a practical learning floor, not statistical proof or a no-go gate.
10. If 50 clean purchases accumulate through multiple small cells rather than one repeatable loop, report that truth; Phase 4 repeatability remains unresolved and the product keeps improving.

No plan can guarantee ten or fifty buyers or a time window. Acquisition failure is classified at the exact funnel layer; the product's development remains fixed.

## Final Answer

Acquire the Initial Design ICP by proving the hard controlled outcome at the moment the Banker already feels the problem. Launch **Trigger Search-to-Control Proof** first, **Artifact Utility-to-Workspace** second, and **Recorded Control-Loop Proof Distribution** third. All three routes converge on the same no-signup synthetic Project Northstar control loop, implemented trust evidence, exact price/qualification preview, direct card checkout, paid authority/confidentiality preflight and guided real first-value path.

Retain the user through the continuing Deal lifecycle—new Source Records, Impact Assessments, deterministic closure, immutable Revisions, Package Readiness, exports, archive/reactivation and new Deals. Earn referral through public synthetic proof or explicit account links; never through leaked Deal Materials or automatic marketing inside confidential artifacts. Treat every traffic, conversion, activation, retention and expansion result as this product's own instrumented evidence, not a conclusion borrowed from competitor pages.

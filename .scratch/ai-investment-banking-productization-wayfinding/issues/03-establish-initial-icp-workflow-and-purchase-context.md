# Establish the Initial ICP's Workflow and Self-Serve Purchase Context

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

How does the confirmed Initial Design ICP currently execute high-value deal work, which jobs and deliverables create the greatest individual economic value, what triggers an independent software purchase, and what evidence would make a premium self-serve purchase credible?

Research overseas boutique investment bankers, small and midsize M&A advisors, and independent transaction advisors using public and authoritative evidence. Cover workflow cadence, time cost, failure cost, existing tools, file and data behavior, purchasing authority, self-serve expectations, price evidence, and repeat-use conditions. Competitors may supply workflow, pricing, and product-pattern evidence but must not be used as exclusion criteria. Produce a linked Markdown research asset without selecting the first Deal workflow.

## Answer

Resolved in [Initial ICP Workflow and Self-Serve Purchase Context](../assets/initial-icp-workflow-and-self-serve-purchase-context.md).

The operational Initial Design ICP is an execution-oriented Individual Banker who personally owns or reviews deal work across repeated revisions and has both self-serve purchase authority and permission to use the intended data. Job title, ability to enter a card, and authority to use confidential Deal Materials are separate qualifications.

The researched workflow is an event-driven, multi-month chain of source collection, analysis, native artifact production, review, circulation, and revision. The durable premium value mechanism is preserving the controlled chain from source → evidence → assumption → calculation → artifact → review or decision across repeated Deal state changes. Public adjacent-product pricing and checkout mechanics support carrying the premium self-serve premise forward, but do not establish this product's launch price, willingness to pay, billing metric, activation workflow, retention, support load, or refund policy.

A credible no-sales purchase therefore requires inspectable product proof: exact file compatibility and preflight, a sanitized runnable Deal Workspace, source and calculation traceability, native Office/PDF outputs, visible human-control gates, product-specific data controls, transparent pricing and limits, and explicit support, cancellation, refund, export, and deletion behavior.

No new ticket or graduated fog is required. Existing downstream tickets already own first-workflow selection, product form, information and evidence controls, monetization, acquisition, and launch validation.

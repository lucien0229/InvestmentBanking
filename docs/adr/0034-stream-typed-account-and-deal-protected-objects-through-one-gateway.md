# Stream typed Account and Deal protected objects through one gateway

V1 stores immutable application-encrypted bytes under an explicit Account or Deal scope and streams both Protected Account Objects and Protected Deal Objects through the same dedicated Protected Object Gateway. The control plane must authorize one exact principal, typed domain attachment, protected object, purpose, operation, current posture, and applicable immutable Revision or Account attachment before issuing a short-lived Stream Grant; the Gateway may revalidate, read, unwrap, and stream only that scope and cannot list objects or mutate domain state. Account-level attachments are closed and typed, including Account Reusable Template versions, retained invoice copies, and Account Data Export objects; they do not create a generic attachment mechanism or allow cross-Deal or live-Deal-to-template reuse. This supersedes ADR 0024 and ADR 0027 only where their wording limited the protected-object encryption and Gateway boundary to Deal-scoped objects, while preserving immutable envelope encryption, no reusable public URL, and separation of business authority from byte decryption.

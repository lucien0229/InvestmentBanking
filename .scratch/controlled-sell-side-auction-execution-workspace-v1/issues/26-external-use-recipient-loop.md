# 26 — Authorize, deliver and observe one exact external Revision

**What to build:** Let the Banker authorize one exact circulation-candidate Revision for one exact recipient/audience/purpose/channel/time/condition scope, explicitly create the corresponding Externally Authorized Delivery and navigation-isolated Recipient Access, and record actual use separately when the verified Recipient first views the exact Reader Copy.

**Blocked by:** 13 — Complete First Unmistakable Value through a Sensitive-Action-protected Internal Export; 20 — Promote a complete Preparation Package without a scalar score.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Creating, revoking, or replacing an External-Use Decision and creating or revoking Recipient Access each require a fresh exact Sensitive Action Grant proven through the Ticket 13 seam; ordinary document review or lifecycle commands do not consume that grant path.
- [ ] External-Use Decision matching verifies exact Deliverable Revision, native/reader hashes, audience, purpose, channel/time, conditions, limitations, decision-maker, authority basis, and active invalidation state.
- [ ] A changed Revision, hash, audience, purpose, condition, source perimeter, or material issue cannot reuse the prior Decision even when Deliverable identity or filename is unchanged.
- [ ] Authorization, Externally Authorized Delivery, Recipient Access creation, Recipient verification/session, and Actual External Use event remain separate immutable states and events.
- [ ] The product performs no autonomous sending, disclosure, contact, access grant, or external action; every delivery/access command requires exact Banker authority and current gates.
- [ ] The navigation-free Recipient viewer exposes only the exact authorized Reader Copy and permitted metadata, with no Deal Workspace, other Buyers, internal Evidence/notes, Native Artifact, or unauthorized Revision.
- [ ] Recipient verification, expiry, revocation/invalidation, no-download stream, first-view event, rate limit, safe denial, and existence-hiding behaviors pass positive and adversarial tests.
- [ ] Browser, HTTP, stream, manifest, Audit, and history observers satisfy AC-060 through AC-063 and ADR 0001.

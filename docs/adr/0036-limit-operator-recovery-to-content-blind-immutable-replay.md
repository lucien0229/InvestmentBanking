# Limit operator recovery to content-blind immutable replay

V1 permits an authenticated Deployment Operator to trigger Recovery Replay or Provider Reconciliation only for an existing immutable Inbox Event, Outbox row, or Job identity through a purpose-built audited procedure. Recovery Replay is permitted only while that identity retains an exact versioned replayable input; where it does not, the procedure may perform only an adapter-defined current-provider-object reconciliation and must label that result as reconciliation rather than historical replay. The procedure binds operator, release, exact target, reason, replay posture, and outcome; re-applies current authorization, version, capability, and idempotency checks; and exposes no Deal content. It cannot create or edit provider evidence, payloads, Job inputs, provider responses, domain records, or selected results; enter a Banker or Recipient session; bypass a control gate; or create Product Entitlement, Human Decision, Recipient Access, External-Use Event, deletion authority, or another business posture. This preserves mechanical recovery without creating a hidden support or database-operator authority path, accepting that cases requiring unavailable input, changed input, judgment, or authorization must return to a provider reconciliation or product-owned user flow instead of being repaired directly by operations.

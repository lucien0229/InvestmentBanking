## Question

Which official end-to-end Investment Banking workflow should anchor the Individual-First Release, and what complete premium outcome should an Individual Banker obtain from one Deal Workspace?

Synthesize the verified Official Capability Baseline, Founder Operating Envelope, and Initial Design ICP workflow evidence. Select for launch sequencing rather than market permission: the answer must name the workflow, triggering situation, controlling inputs, complete Core Business Workflow, hero outcome, repeat-use loop, and why the outcome can justify Premium Product Value through Self-Serve Purchase. Preserve the full product vision and do not treat competition as a veto or search only for uncovered features. Produce a linked decision asset; do not define the detailed interface or final V1 feature list.

## Answer

Resolved by [First End-to-End Deal Workflow and Premium Hero Outcome](../assets/first-deal-workflow-and-premium-hero-outcome.md).

The Individual-First Release will anchor on the official **Sell-side auction** workflow. Its Premium Hero Outcome is the **Controlled Sell-Side Auction Deal Book**: one persistent Deal Workspace that converts an authorized source packet and the Individual Banker's approved judgments into a senior-review-ready, continuously refreshable sell-side execution package, preserves the source → evidence → assumption → calculation → native artifact → review/decision chain through launch, buyer/process/bid/diligence events, and reaches circulation-candidate status only after explicit human approval and applicable QC gates.

The asset assesses all ten official routes for launch sequencing. Model update is embedded as the first workflow's refresh foundation rather than launched as a point tool; Sponsor buy-side is the first major workflow expansion; committee and board routes follow validated upstream analysis; capital-markets and specialized fairness/restructuring routes expand after their market-data, credit/covenant, committee, and specialist-control primitives exist.

AI and deterministic engines may execute, draft, reconcile, calculate, refresh, and check. They may not silently approve material definitions, assumptions, buyer/process judgments, legal or other specialist conclusions, external action, or circulation. The Individual Banker retains professional judgment and every external-use decision; the provider supplies no human banker review or high-touch Deal service.

No new ticket or fog item is required. Existing downstream tickets own the Deal Workspace model, data/confidentiality contract, AI/evidence/human-control contract, deliverable quality, self-serve prototype, monetization, acquisition, and final V1 `/to-spec` boundary.

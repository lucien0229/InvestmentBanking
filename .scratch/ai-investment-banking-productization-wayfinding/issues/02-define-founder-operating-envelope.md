# Define the Founder Operating Envelope for a Premium Self-Serve Product

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Which founder-specific resources, constraints, advantages, risk tolerances, and timing requirements must the Productization Blueprint respect?

Resolve this through live, one-question-at-a-time grilling. Establish available team and skills, weekly time, validation and build budget, acceptable model and data costs, target geography and language, access to bankers, acceptable support burden, security posture, time to first chargeable release, revenue expectations, and any non-negotiable implementation or operating constraints. Preserve the confirmed product-will-be-built premise and do not turn these constraints into a go/no-go decision.

## Answer

The confirmed Founder Operating Envelope is a solo, long-term, full-time founder building a premium Self-Serve Purchase product without founder-led discovery, founder sales, banker advisors, high-touch service, fixed budget or cost limits, a release deadline, or a revenue target. These are product-design and operating boundaries, not go/no-go gates: the Independent Product will be built, and the Official Capability Baseline remains its minimum reference rather than a competitor.

### Founder and available capabilities

- The team is exactly one long-term, full-time founder. No fixed employees are planned.
- The founder can independently own full-stack Web SaaS development and deployment, AI workflow and model API integration, document processing and Office-file generation, basic product and UX design, basic cloud operations, and incident handling.
- There is no senior Investment Banking advisor. The founder does not provide manual Investment Banking review.
- The product provider relies on AI for workflow execution, workflow-reality checks, output-quality checks, and boundary warnings. The AI may execute and inspect work, but it may not silently approve material assumptions, professional judgments, or external circulation.
- The Individual Banker owns the final approval of material assumptions, professional judgment, and external use. The product must not claim that AI-generated work is independently banker-approved.
- The product does not conduct user interviews or founder-led sales.

### Capacity, budget, cost, timing, and revenue

- Founder commitment is long-term and full-time, but no weekly-hour number or time allocation is imposed.
- There is no validation-budget or build-budget ceiling before the first chargeable release.
- Model, data, document-processing, storage, compute, and infrastructure costs are not currently constrained by an absolute amount, cost ratio, or gross-margin threshold.
- There is no fixed deadline or target number of weeks to the first chargeable release. The product should charge only when it supports real Self-Serve Purchase and use; speed must not reduce it to a preorder page, demo, prompt wrapper, or founder-delivered service.
- There is no user-count, MRR, ARR, payback, income-replacement, or other revenue expectation in this envelope. Later monetization work may define price, billing unit, direct-cost logic, and commercial metrics without reopening whether the product will be built.

### Validation and access to bankers

- The founder has no banker network, design partners, advisory board, warm-introduction channel, or institutional partnership on which the Blueprint may depend.
- Product validation must not require banker interviews, co-design, founder-led demos, manual pilots, or founder-assisted closing.
- Validation may rely on real Self-Serve Purchase and product behavior, including activation, use, repeat use, retention, refunds, support requests, and voluntarily submitted asynchronous feedback.
- Workflow knowledge and initial quality calibration may draw on the Official Capability Baseline, authoritative public materials, lawfully obtained sample files, AI reasoning, deterministic checks, and the Individual Banker's final control.

### Geography and language

- The United States is the primary launch market.
- English is the only officially supported product, help, input-understanding, and deliverable language for the first chargeable release.
- Users elsewhere may purchase and use the product self-serve, but the release does not promise localization, jurisdiction-specific workflows, terminology, data sources, or regulatory adaptation.
- United States targeting does not imply that the product provides legal, regulatory, fairness, solvency, or other regulated professional opinions.

### Support and service boundary

- Support is asynchronous through email or in-product tickets.
- Self-serve onboarding, examples, documentation, and recovery guidance must carry routine adoption and troubleshooting.
- The founder may handle account, billing, product-defect, and unrecoverable product issues.
- There is no implementation service, synchronous training, Customer Success, 24/7 support, formal SLA, manual Deal Materials cleanup, workflow operation on the user's behalf, deliverable completion service, custom consulting, or provider-side Investment Banking review.
- Product gaps should become reusable product improvements rather than one-off manual fulfillment.

### Security and compliance posture

- Enterprise security and compliance programs are deferred: the first release does not require SOC 2, ISO 27001, third-party certification, formal compliance staffing, SSO, SCIM, selectable data residency, information barriers, DLP, granular team permissions, or enterprise procurement materials.
- The product must not market itself as certified, regulator-approved, or institution-grade merely because it has baseline controls.
- Before accepting real or confidential Deal Materials, it must provide minimum technical controls: authenticated access, tenant data isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, appropriate secret and key handling, data deletion, basic audit records, and model/API configurations that do not default to training public models on customer data.
- Until those minimum controls exist, the product may process only public, synthetic, or de-identified materials.
- The Individual Banker remains responsible for having authority to upload and use the materials supplied to the product.

### Non-negotiable product and operating constraints

1. The product will be built; this envelope cannot become a go/no-go test.
2. The official Investment Banking plugin is the minimum Official Capability Baseline, not a competitor or a feature gap to evade.
3. The operating model remains one long-term, full-time founder with no fixed employees.
4. AI performs provider-side workflow execution and checking; the Individual Banker retains final professional and external-use control.
5. The founder supplies no human Investment Banking review.
6. There are no user interviews, founder sales, warm-introduction dependencies, design-partner dependencies, co-design engagements, or founder-operated pilots.
7. Discovery, evaluation, purchase, onboarding, and first value must work through Self-Serve Purchase.
8. Support stays asynchronous and productized; there is no implementation, training, Customer Success, manual fulfillment, or bespoke consulting.
9. The launch focus is the United States and the supported language is English.
10. The Individual-First Release completes one coherent Core Business Workflow inside a persistent Deal Workspace.
11. The product is not a generic chatbot or a menu of disconnected AI tools.
12. Advanced collaboration, granular permissions, approval routing, organization administration, custom deployment, and mandatory enterprise procurement remain deferred.
13. Weekly hours, validation and build budgets, model/data/infrastructure costs, release timing, and revenue expectations are deliberately unconstrained.
14. Enterprise security and compliance are deferred, but confidential Deal Materials are prohibited until the minimum technical security boundary is met.
15. Premium Product Value must come from completing important banker work, not feature count, provider-side human service, or mandatory enterprise sales.
16. The first chargeable release must be a usable product, not a preorder, demo, prompt wrapper, or consulting engagement.
17. Later tickets may decide the first workflow, product mechanics, data contract, monetization, and acquisition system, but may not silently relax this envelope.

No new ticket or fog item is required. The existing Wayfinder tickets already own the remaining ICP, workflow-selection, Deal Workspace, data, AI-control, deliverable, prototype, monetization, acquisition, and final-blueprint decisions.

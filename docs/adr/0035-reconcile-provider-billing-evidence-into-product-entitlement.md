# Reconcile provider billing evidence into product-owned entitlement

V1 treats Stripe events, objects, statuses, Checkout redirects, and Customer Portal actions as provider evidence rather than product authority, and converts them through a durable Webhook Inbox, versioned Provider Reconciliation, and idempotent product transaction into Commercial Receipts, Payment Disputes, Billing Recovery, Refund Effects, Entitlement Mutations, and the current Product Entitlement. A scheduled renewal failure while an already-paid term remains current enters Billing Recovery: the Account keeps only the term already paid through, receives no new period, add-on, or capacity, and begins the confirmed 30-day Post-Term Access if failure remains unresolved at the paid-through boundary. An open Payment Dispute preserves Account-owner inspection, Internal Controlled Export, billing correction, and deletion while blocking new substantive processing, capacity purchase, External-Use Decisions, Externally Authorized Deliveries, and new Recipient Access; the previously deferred treatment of already-active Recipient Access is resolved by [ADR 0037](0037-suspend-reversible-recipient-access-restrictions.md), which suspends rather than revokes it and requires explicit exact resumption after a won or reversed dispute. A lost dispute ends the affected paid term and revokes all Recipient Access before Post-Term Access. A provider refund changes entitlement only through a closed product-owned Refund Effect: the first-Deal guarantee or an exact paid-term reversal may end the affected term, a duplicate-charge correction or tax/invoice adjustment preserves it, an unused add-on refund reverses only that capacity, and an unmapped refund changes nothing until reconciled. V1 adds no accounting, general-ledger, or revenue-recognition write-back authority, accepting additional reconciliation records and temporary restricted states so duplicated, reordered, ambiguous, or externally changed provider data cannot silently create product capability.

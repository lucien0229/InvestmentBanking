# Controlled Sell-Side Auction Execution Workspace V1

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

An execution-oriented Individual Banker running a Sell-Side Auction must turn an authorized, changing body of management materials, financial files, diligence records, buyer and process information, Bids, and professional judgments into current analysis, banker-native workbooks and materials, review decisions, and controlled external-use actions. Today that work is fragmented across files, trackers, messages, calculations, drafts, and manual review. When a source, assumption, Bid, process event, or audience changes, the Banker must determine which facts, calculations, workbook cells, slides, paragraphs, Reviews, readiness conclusions, and prior authorizations are now stale or invalid.

The Banker cannot safely solve this problem with a generic chatbot, a one-shot document generator, a disconnected menu of AI tools, or a static file repository. A fluent answer may still contain an unsupported Claim, the wrong period or unit, a stale spreadsheet cache, an unresolved conflict, a broken native artifact, a mismatched Reader Copy, confidential information intended for another audience, or a prior External-Use Decision that no longer applies. A generated file that opens is not necessarily mechanically valid, professionally usable, senior-review-ready, circulation-candidate, or authorized for external use.

The workflow is a continuing Deal lifecycle rather than a single generation task. It begins with Deal initiation and Preparation, continues through In Market, Bid Evaluation, Exclusive Execution, Signed and Closed or Terminated, and must also support Paused, Archived, reactivated, backward-moving, and repeatedly revised Deals. The same Deal identity and history must survive each change without silent overwrite or reconstruction from chat history.

The Banker needs one authoritative Deal Workspace that preserves the distinction between Source Material, Source Records, Evidence, Claims, Facts, Assumptions, Calculations, Models, Scenarios, Analyses, Process Events, Deliverables, Revisions, Reviews, QC Findings, Human Decisions, and External-Use Decisions. In particular, Source Material is not Evidence, Evidence is not a Fact, an approved Assumption remains an Assumption, Mechanical Validity is not Professional Usability, readiness is not authorization, and authorization is not proof that an external action occurred.

The product must remain useful when the source packet is incomplete, stale, conflicted, rights-limited, unsupported, or only partially parsed. Those conditions must produce an explicit output ceiling, source request, approved Assumption lane, sensitivity-only result, or blocker. They must never invite AI to invent a missing Fact, silently select a favorable source, or hide the effect on downstream work.

The product must produce the editable formats expected in banker work. The premium result is not a PDF or dashboard alone; it is a Controlled Auction Execution Package containing two workbook spines, stage-triggered native marketing and decision materials, exact Reader Copies, evidence and control records, Package Readiness, and an immutable archive/export package. Native editability, source-to-artifact lineage, deterministic recalculation, render parity, protected Banker edits, and round-trip behavior are part of the product contract.

The Individual Banker must be able to discover, evaluate, purchase, qualify, onboard, operate, export, and leave the product without mandatory sales, implementation, live training, or provider-side Banker review. Purchase authority and authority to upload or use Deal Materials are separate. Real Confidential or Restricted Deal Materials must remain blocked until the product's minimum security, confidentiality, retention, export, deletion, provider-processing, and isolation controls are implemented and verified.

The commercial problem is equally specific: the product must sell a complete Individual Deal Desk, not AI credits or a crippled lead-in tier, while keeping high-cost source processing economically legible. It must preserve unmetered evidence inspection, correction, deterministic validation, QC, review, Human Decisions, normal targeted Revisions, product-failure recovery, and normal exports. Willingness to pay, conversion, usage, support load, margins, retention, and acquisition-channel performance remain post-launch validation questions; they do not reopen whether this product will be built.

## Solution

Build an independent, United States-first, English Web App for an execution-oriented Individual Banker. The product is a controlled Sell-Side Auction execution workspace that turns an authorized Source Packet into one persistent Controlled Sell-Side Auction Deal Book and a continuously refreshable Controlled Auction Execution Package. It delivers the complete Individual-First Sell-Side Auction workflow from Initiated and Preparation through In Market, Bid Evaluation, Exclusive Execution, Signed and Closed or Terminated, with Paused, Archived, reactivation, backward transitions, and continuing immutable Revisions.

The Initial Design ICP is the confirmed execution-oriented Individual Banker at an overseas boutique investment bank, small or midsize M&A advisory firm, or independent transaction advisory practice who can independently purchase the product, owns day-to-day Sell-Side Auction execution, and can separately confirm authority to use the relevant Deal Materials.

The user experience follows the confirmed asymmetric C → A → B product form:

- C owns outcome-first public discovery. A prospective buyer can inspect a no-signup synthetic Project Northstar Deal, exact source locators, a material conflict, an extraction correction, deterministic recovery, affected native and reader artifacts, Package Readiness, Revision history, and authorization consequences before providing Deal Materials.
- A owns guided first correct use inside the paid product. A recoverable First Deal Guide sequences Deal identity, authority, confidentiality, Source Packet selection, AI and deterministic responsibilities, Evidence inspection, typed Banker decisions, applicable deterministic validation, downstream impact, artifact review, export, and graduation.
- B is the durable Deal Execution Desk. It becomes the normal shell for Deal Brief, Event Inbox, Sources, Work Queue, Evidence and Decisions, Analysis, Execution Package, QC and Readiness, History and Export, archive, and reactivation. C remains available inside B as the Execution Package and Package Readiness view.

The product uses three explicit responsibility planes. AI may ingest, extract, classify, compare, propose, analyze, draft, explain, detect issues, and recommend while preserving Origin, scope, lineage, uncertainty, and blockers. Deterministic procedures perform or validate reproducible arithmetic, formulas, recalculation, tie-outs, units, periods, currency, signs, schemas, versions, dependencies, process-state rules, citations, cross-artifact consistency, and isolation rules. The Individual Banker alone accepts material Claims as Facts, approves material Assumptions, resolves material conflicts, decides professional suitability and materiality, approves Buyers and process actions, selects Bids and recommendations, and authorizes exact external use.

The product persists one Deal Workspace per Deal. It preserves immutable Source Records and Deliverable Revisions, typed Evidence and decision relationships, append-only Process Events and audit history, independent Source, Analysis, Deliverable, and Process states, and Impact Assessments that identify recalculation, regeneration, re-review, and circulation-blocking consequences. A change never overwrites the prior state or silently carries forward Review, readiness, or authorization.

The Controlled Auction Execution Package contains an always-current Analysis and Valuation Workbook and Auction Control Workbook; stage-triggered Teaser and CIM in PPTX with exact PDF Reader Copies; a stage-triggered Bid Evaluation and Recommendation Memo in DOCX with an exact PDF Reader Copy; conditional Management Presentation, update deck, process letter, and supporting schedules when applicable; machine-trackable Evidence and control records; Package Readiness; and an index-first immutable archive/export package. Stage-inapplicable artifacts are marked not stage-required rather than manufactured prematurely.

The first sellable release includes the complete security, Evidence, file-control, guided first-use, Preparation, In-Market process, Bid Evaluation, Exclusive Execution, lifecycle, archive, and return-Revision behavior. It directly productizes buyer/investor list, CIM builder, comps valuation, deal process tracking, financial normalization, deck QC, and memo building. It uses CIM teardown, company tearsheet, DCF, meeting prep, model audit and tie-out, pitch-deck architecture, scenario and sensitivity generation, and three-statement modeling as bounded capabilities inside the Sell-Side Auction. Deferred official workflows remain part of the long-term Official Capability Baseline but are not V1 routes.

The complete Individual Deal Desk costs $995 per month or $10,950 per year paid upfront for one named Individual Banker and two concurrent Active Deal Workspaces. Additional Active Deal capacity costs $500 per month or $5,500 per year. Each Active Deal receives the confirmed file, logical-page, storage, and full-workflow-operation envelope. Exception processing and archive capacity use the confirmed explicit packs. Professional-control actions and product-failure recovery remain unmetered. The conditional 14-day First-Deal Control-Loop Guarantee applies exactly to supported, authorized, preflighted product-side failure before the defined milestone.

Acquisition begins with Trigger Search-to-Control Proof, then Artifact Utility-to-Workspace, then Recorded Control-Loop Proof Distribution. All routes converge on the same synthetic proof, exact price and qualification, direct checkout, paid authority/confidentiality preflight, guided first value, persistent Deal Execution Desk, lifecycle retention, and confidentiality-safe referral or future Team signal. Market-facing claims are enabled only when their implementation prerequisites are true and verified.

V1 succeeds when an eligible Individual Banker can complete the public proof, purchase without assistance, pass paid preflight, create an exact Deal, accept a minimum Source Packet, inspect exact Evidence, record a typed material decision, complete applicable deterministic validation, see the affected Native Artifact, Reader Copy, QC, readiness, and authorization consequence, export the exact Revision, operate the complete Sell-Side Auction lifecycle, and return after a new material Source Record or Process Event to produce a new immutable Revision without losing history or carrying authorization forward.

## Implementation Decisions

### Authority and epistemic contract

- The canonical vocabulary and business invariants are those defined by the repository Domain Context. Implementation names may be more technical internally, but every user-facing state, event, test, and ticket must preserve the canonical terms rather than invent synonyms that collapse distinctions.
- Product requirements in the confirmed Blueprint and resolved Wayfinder decisions are binding. The official Investment Banking plugin is the minimum capability baseline and a source of workflow, Evidence, calculation, artifact, and handoff expectations; it is not a production runtime dependency, competitor, PMF proof, WTP proof, security proof, or production-readiness proof.
- Four epistemic classes remain visible in implementation and product documentation: Verified fact, Evidence-backed inference, Product design decision, and Unresolved implementation/evaluation question. A provider statement, competitor mechanism, synthetic fixture, AI output, or prototype observation may not be silently promoted to a Verified fact about the production product.
- Product behavior must retain Claim, Fact, Assumption, Calculation, Analysis, and Recommendation as distinct object types with distinct transitions and Evidence burdens. No generic content object or confidence field may collapse them.
- Product behavior must retain Mechanical Validity, Professional Usability, Deliverable Readiness, External-Use Decision, and actual external-use Process Event as independent conditions. No global `approved`, `ready`, or `complete` flag may substitute for them.
- Time-sensitive vendor, model, API, pricing, retention, region, parser, renderer, payment, tax, regulatory, and plugin facts are implementation inputs only after current verification. The spec does not freeze a provider or repeat the prior plugin test snapshot as a current production fact.

### Architectural shape and system boundaries

- V1 will use one product control plane with separated background worker processes rather than a network of independently owned product microservices. The control plane owns authenticated commands, business invariants, current state, entitlement, and user-visible job status. Workers own bounded parsing, OCR, AI, deterministic calculation, native artifact generation, rendering, comparison, export, and evaluation jobs.
- The authoritative business state will be stored in a transactional relational system of record. Immutable source and artifact bytes, rendered representations, and archive packages will be stored in versioned object storage. Durable jobs will use a persistent queue. User-visible state changes and side effects will use a transactional outbox or equivalent atomic publication mechanism so that accepted commands cannot be lost between state commit and job/event dispatch.
- V1 will not require full event sourcing. Current object state may be stored directly, but every Source Record, Process Event, material object version, Human Decision, Deliverable Revision, Impact Assessment, QC run, External-Use Decision, entitlement mutation, export, and deletion event must have an append-only historical record sufficient to reconstruct who changed what, when, why, under which source and version perimeter, and with what downstream result.
- Every externally retryable command and job will have an idempotency identity. Duplicate browser submissions, payment webhooks, queue delivery, provider callbacks, retries, and worker restarts must not create duplicate Source Records, Facts, Decisions, Revisions, charges, allowance consumption, exports, or deletion requests.
- Long-running work is asynchronous. The product accepts or rejects the command synchronously, records the exact input perimeter and expected work, and exposes queued, running, waiting-for-user, waiting-for-source, blocked, failed-retryable, failed-terminal, canceled, and completed states as applicable. One aggregate progress percentage is not authoritative.
- Provider integrations are behind replaceable boundary adapters for model execution, OCR/parsing, calculation, native rendering, PDF generation, payment, email, storage, observability, and security tooling. Provider test doubles may inject deterministic success, failure, timeout, retry, malformed response, retention-incompatible, and cost conditions. The product contract is expressed in provider-independent business results.
- V1 has no external-action adapter capable of sending Deal communication, contacting a Buyer, accepting a Bid, granting Data-Room Access, mutating a VDR, or executing another Deal action. Export and recipient-specific read-only sharing are controlled product outputs, not autonomous external execution.

### Identity, isolation, confidentiality, and security

- Real Confidential and Restricted Deal Materials are disabled until authenticated access, account isolation, Deal isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, secret separation, safe provider configuration, audit/provenance, self-serve export and deletion, accurate subprocessor/retention/training/support-access disclosure, and negative isolation tests are implemented and verified together.
- Authorization is enforced at every access layer: user command, business service, database query, job payload, object-storage key, cache, search or retrieval index, model request, render job, export, and notification. A Deal ID in the UI or prompt is not an authorization control.
- Each Source Record, derived object, job, artifact, render, export, Decision, and audit event carries immutable account and Deal scope. Cross-Deal retrieval is denied by default even for the same Individual Banker. Reusable templates or workflow structure may cross Deals only when their rights and content classification permit it; confidential facts, embeddings, prompts, outputs, and Decisions may not.
- Uploaded content passes file-integrity, supported-type, archive safety, active-content, and malware policy before substantive parsing. Unsafe content is quarantined or rejected without being sent to an AI or renderer. Passwords are not stored; users must supply an authorized unlocked copy.
- Credentials and provider secrets are stored outside Deal Materials, prompts, logs, Source Records, exports, and analytics. Any future delegated connector uses least-privilege scoped credentials, but no connector is required or implemented by this spec.
- Customer Confidential or Restricted Deal Materials are not used to train a shared product or foundation model by default. Feedback, support, analytics, tracing, evaluation, and provider dashboards must not silently opt content into training or broader retention.
- Provider routing checks the exact source classification and recorded restrictions against the endpoint's verified training, retention, application-state, region, and support-access posture before dispatch. An incompatible path produces a blocker or permitted deterministic/manual-only lane.
- V1 has no support-personnel role, Banker impersonation, administrative Deal viewer, or content-level break-glass path. Deployment Operators use privacy-safe runtime metadata and cannot enter a Banker or Recipient session, inspect or decrypt Deal Material, change domain state, or copy content into operational tooling. Content-specific diagnosis uses bounded user-controlled retry, re-upload, recovery, or Internal Controlled Export paths.
- Logs, traces, analytics, alert payloads, support metadata, and transactional emails contain pseudonymous identifiers and safe reason codes only. They exclude client, company, Buyer and Deal names; filenames and paths; source text; extracted values; formulas; Bids; Human Decision text; artifact excerpts; recipient lists; and confidential milestone detail.
- Archive is not deletion. A deletion request removes normal user and product access immediately, cancels new work, records the deletion perimeter, completes deletion from active systems within 30 days, and allows ordinary encrypted backup copies to expire within 90 days unless a disclosed preservation obligation applies. Minimal non-content billing, security, or legal records remain only for a stated period and purpose.
- Account closure provides the confirmed 30-day read-only export window unless the user requests immediate deletion. New source processing, AI work, Calculations, Revisions, and readiness advancement are disabled during the window.

### Public proof, checkout, and guided first value

- Project Northstar is a production-quality public proof surface built from synthetic, rights-cleared fixture data. The throwaway prototype supplies interaction decisions only and is not a code, styling, architecture, persistence, security, payment, or artifact-generation foundation.
- Public proof completion requires one resumable session to open exact source lineage, complete the synthetic correction and conflict disposition, observe deterministic recovery, inspect at least one affected Native Artifact and Reader Copy, inspect Package Readiness and authorization consequences, and view the export and Revision boundary. Passive animation, a screenshot, or a video impression does not count.
- Essential proof, methodology, pricing, qualification, guarantee, security/data-use disclosures, and synthetic downloads remain available without email or card. An optional account may save synthetic progress but may not gate these objects.
- The pre-payment qualification preview collects no real Confidential Deal Materials. It exposes supported formats, current compatibility classes, operational envelope, minimum Source Packet, authority questions, intended-use boundaries, known unsupported behavior, and the security facts required for a qualified purchase.
- Successful payment creates account entitlement and the two Active Deal slots. Before real upload, paid preflight records purchase authority, exact Deal identity and use, source/use authority, confidentiality, processing-path compatibility, file and template posture, minimum security status, minimum Source Packet status, and guarantee eligibility.
- The First Deal Guide is a mode inside the Deal Workspace. It is resumable, reopenable, and records completed, blocked, waiting, and recovered steps. It does not create a second onboarding data model.
- First unmistakable value is recorded only when one real supported Deal has exact Source Record and Evidence inspection, one material typed Banker correction or Fact/Assumption/Claim/conflict Decision, applicable deterministic validation, affected Native Artifact and exact Reader Copy result or correct blocker, visible Impact Assessment/QC/Package Readiness/authorization consequence, and no unresolved product-side failure preventing that result.
- A real Deal without a genuine source conflict or extraction error uses a normal material proposition review and valid deterministic check. The product never injects a false exception to satisfy activation or guarantee criteria.
- After First Unmistakable Value, the first permitted Internal Controlled Export proves exact portability without creating external-use authority. Completion of that export plus explicit entry graduates the user into the persistent Deal Execution Desk, changing the primary navigation and next-action surface without changing the Deal identity or stored objects. The First Deal Guide and its history remain accessible.

### Deal, lifecycle, and state behavior

- A Deal's durable identity is determined only by the client or represented party, transaction subject and perimeter, Banker role or side, and mandate objective. A material change to any identity element creates a new linkable Deal. Changes to source versions, Bids, monthly results, process path, stage, pause, restart, or exclusivity remain within the same Deal when identity is unchanged.
- One Deal has one Deal Workspace. The Controlled Sell-Side Auction Deal Book is its governed current execution view. The Controlled Auction Execution Package is the applicable Deliverable family inside that Deal Book. Neither creates a second source of truth.
- The canonical business-stage path is Initiated, Preparation, In Market, Bid Evaluation, Exclusive Execution, Signed, and Closed, with Terminated as a distinct end state. Paused is an independent activity posture and Archived is an independent record posture.
- Stage transitions require a valid transition command, applicable Human Decision, and supporting Process Event or Evidence. AI may recommend a transition but may not apply it. In Market requires an occurred authorized outreach event; Signed requires the controlling definitive-agreement event; Closed requires the completion event.
- Backward transitions are permitted where the real process requires them. Every transition appends history and records its supporting Decision and Process Event. No transition deletes or rewrites prior stage history.
- Active Deal Workspace permits new Source Records, material analysis or package work, Revisions, and Package Readiness advancement. Archived Deal Workspace is read-only for search, Evidence inspection, download, export, and deletion. Reactivation returns to an explicit stage, consumes an Active Deal slot, and preserves the same Deal history.
- Source Reliance State, Analysis State, Deliverable Readiness, Process State, Deal Business Stage, information freshness, conflict, disposition, Origin, and Human Confirmation are separate dimensions. Package Readiness may aggregate them for action but may not store a contradictory independent truth.
- Buyer Candidate, Approved Buyer, Outreach Wave, actual outreach, interest, NDA, disclosure, Data-Room Access, Bid receipt, selection, exclusivity, signing, and closing remain separate. The product does not infer an event from a plan, silence, schedule, AI recommendation, or tracker label alone.
- Diligence Issue, Information Request, and Open Item remain independent. A Diligence Issue changes state only through its resolution criteria and explicit Banker disposition, not because linked requests or tasks closed.
- Every material Source Record, Process Event, Fact, Assumption, Model, Scenario, method, Bid, Human Decision, audience, purpose, template, or renderer change triggers an Impact Assessment against current dependent objects.
- An Impact Assessment records each dependent as unaffected, potentially affected, materially affected, or unable to assess, and sets Recalculation Required, Regeneration Required, Re-review Required, and Circulation Blocked independently. Deterministic lineage finds candidate dependents; AI may propose semantic impact; the Banker owns material professional disposition.

### Source, Evidence, AI, and deterministic behavior

- Source receipt, parsing, OCR/table recognition, normalization, Evidence eligibility, Source Reliance, and downstream readiness are separate states. The product preserves the original representation and every derived version.
- Source Record control includes identity, Deal, origin, acquisition method, authority basis, rights, confidentiality, dates, version/draft/executed posture, content identity, supported-format status, parse/OCR/table/visual coverage, native locators, freshness, conflict, disposition, reliance state, and dependent-object edges.
- Rights and confidentiality propagate from Source Records into Evidence, Claims, Calculations, Models, Analyses, Deliverables, Reader Copies, exports, and provider paths. Derived or summarized content does not become less confidential automatically.
- A Source Packet is a versioned, Banker-controlled set of exact Source Records for a purpose, stage, or decision scope. Adding a Source Record changes the packet version and triggers impact; removing or withdrawing a source preserves the prior packet history.
- Evidence locators are format-specific and version-bound. PDF locators retain page and region or table context; PPTX locators retain slide and object context; XLSX locators retain workbook, sheet and cell/range or structured object context; DOCX locators retain structural paragraph, table, footnote, bookmark, comment, or revision context; CSV locators retain version, stable row key or immutable row, column, and export context; web locators retain a captured representation or digest, URL, access time, document identity, and exact excerpt selector where permitted.
- Claims are atomic, attributed, and falsifiable. Facts require appropriate Evidence, no unresolved material conflict, and a typed Human Decision for a stated scope and purpose. Assumptions require explicit bounds, rationale, allowed use, owner, replacement Evidence, and review trigger.
- AI work receives an exact Deal, source, object, intended-use, and policy perimeter. Its outputs record model/provider and policy version, input object IDs, Origin, result objects, citations, uncertainty dimensions, abstentions, warnings, and cost metadata without persisting confidential prompt text in ordinary analytics.
- AI must abstain or downgrade when rights, confidentiality, source authority, definition, period, unit, coverage, conflict, deterministic validity, or intended-use conditions are insufficient. It may propose a source request, explicit Assumption, alternative case, or narrower output; it may not fill the gap with an unsupported Fact.
- Deterministic calculation owns arithmetic and financial formulas; explicit unit, currency, sign, period, precision, and rounding rules; spreadsheet recalculation; balance, subtotal, bridge, and tie-out invariants; scenario isolation; source/model/artifact consistency; and reproducible output materialization.
- Deterministic validation owns required-field and schema validity; state-transition prerequisites; Source/Deal/version binding; locator and lineage existence; formula/dependency integrity; cached-versus-recalculated status; external-link, circularity, hidden-content, and error inventory; object and hash identity; archive manifest validity; recipient and external-use match; and cross-account/Deal negative isolation.
- A deterministic check record contains exact inputs, object versions, applicable rule set, engine and version, coverage, result, exceptions, timestamp, and downstream blockers removed or created. The product never generalizes a local pass into Professional Usability.
- Human Decisions are immutable records. A later correction creates a new object version and, when necessary, a new Decision. The current pointer may change, but the original AI/imported output, prior Decision, supporting Evidence, and prior downstream use remain historical.

- Bid Evaluation includes exact Bid versions, normalized term comparison, supported valuation and Scenario consequences, controlled recommendation drafting, Bid Evaluation and Recommendation Memo, and explicit Banker selection.

### Deliverable, native artifact, Reader Copy, and round-trip contract

- The Analysis and Valuation Workbook and Auction Control Workbook are always-required Native Artifacts for an Active Sell-Side Auction Deal Workspace. Stage-triggered artifacts are Teaser and CIM in PPTX plus exact PDF Reader Copies, and Bid Evaluation and Recommendation Memo in DOCX plus an exact PDF Reader Copy. Conditional artifacts are produced only when their stage, audience, and purpose make them applicable.
- Every Deliverable is a stable business object with owner, Deal, purpose, stage, audience class, confidentiality, current Revision, history, applicable format contract, dependencies, Reviews, QC Findings, readiness, and Decisions. Filenames are presentation details and cannot establish identity or version.
- Every Revision is immutable. Its record binds semantic content, Native Artifact bytes and hash, Reader Copy bytes and hash when applicable, source/model/process dependencies, template and renderer versions, intended audience and purpose, Review/QC evidence, readiness, and Decisions.
- The Analysis and Valuation Workbook preserves formulas, input and Assumption separation, exact source-to-cell lineage, definitions, periods, units, currency, signs, actual/forecast status, scenarios, tie-outs, charts, calculation mode, engine/version, and cached-versus-recalculated state. Material external links, macros, unsupported functions, circularity, hidden dependencies, or manual calculation limitations cap or block readiness according to the declared support matrix.
- The Auction Control Workbook preserves stable Buyer, process, NDA/access, diligence, request, Bid, issue, Milestone, Decision, and history identities. Planned, proposed, approved, occurred, and current states remain separate. Its first visible tab is an executive control view, not the only source of process truth.
- PPTX artifacts preserve native editable text, tables, charts, masters, layouts, themes, placeholders, notes, comments, footers, page numbers, source zones, and confidentiality zones where supported. Required content may not be flattened into images to claim native editability.
- DOCX artifacts preserve named styles, heading and numbering hierarchy, real tables, sections, headers and footers, page numbers, fields and cross-references where applicable, comments, tracked changes, footnotes, and clean-copy state where supported.
- PDF is a fixed Reader Copy of the exact Native Artifact Revision, not the editable source. The record retains renderer, renderer version, generation time, page/slide/sheet selection, print settings, fonts or substitutions, audience, purpose, confidentiality, content/parity result, and hash.
- File-visible citations serve the reader; machine lineage serves reproducibility, impact, refresh, and audit. Material outputs require both at the applicable readiness gate.
- Template upload is per Deal or artifact class and rights-gated. Template preflight inventories file integrity, active content, links, fonts, masters/layouts/styles, comments/revisions, dimensions, required content-role mappings, unsupported features, expected fidelity, and fallback. V1 provides professional default templates capable of producing the complete Individual value without manual repair.
- When a user template is incompatible, the product offers a named limited-fidelity mapping, the default professional template while retaining the uploaded template as reference, or a blocker until a compatible template is supplied. It never silently flattens or discards native structures.
- External edit and reimport uses a three-way comparison among the prior exported Revision, the externally edited artifact, and the current controlled Revision. Changes are classified as Banker edit, formula/source change, style/layout change, comment/review change, unsupported feature, or conflict. Accepted changes form a new Revision and retain import/author provenance.
- The archive/export package is index-first and includes only the permitted source and audience scope. It lists Deal and Package identity, included Deliverable Revisions, file roles, exact hashes, schemas, templates, renderers, lineage, QC, Decisions, missing or excluded items, and export time. A digest proves byte identity and change detection only; it does not prove correctness, authorship, suitability, or authorization.

### Readiness, QC, external-use, and failure behavior

- Deliverable Readiness is working-draft, analysis-ready, senior-review-ready, circulation-candidate, or blocked for an exact Revision and stated use. Blocked may apply at any attempted gate. There is no `circulated` readiness state.
- Working-draft permits internal construction with explicit gaps. Analysis-ready requires applicable material inputs or approved Assumptions, passed relied-on Calculations and tie-outs, core native editability, and Banker-approved methods for the stated internal analysis. Senior-review-ready additionally requires complete material lineage, applicable native recalculation, cross-artifact tie-out, relevant render inspection, disclosed issues, and a named internal review scope. Circulation-candidate additionally requires exact audience and purpose, complete rights/confidentiality conditions, all applicable native/render/PDF/clean-copy/QC gates, professional suitability, and no circulation-blocking Finding.
- A QC Finding records exact object and Revision location, severity, Evidence, rule or reviewer, impact, owner, remediation state, intended-use consequence, and re-test result. Critical failures block the affected use and, when systemic or present in the release sentinel suite, block release. Major failures block the stated review or circulation posture. Minor failures may remain only when non-material, visible, owned, and accepted for the exact purpose.
- A successful parser, AI output, calculation, build, file open, render, or QC scan advances only the property it proves. It cannot promote a Claim, Professional Usability, readiness, or external use outside that property.
- External-Use Decision preparation is available only for circulation-candidate exact Revisions. The Decision binds artifact hashes, audience or recipient class, purpose, channel/time, conditions, confidentiality, rights, disclosure, required specialist or firm conditions, limitations, authority basis, decision-maker, and invalidation triggers.
- The product does not transmit Deal Materials as a consequence of an External-Use Decision. An actual external use is recorded separately when the Banker supplies an applicable Process Event or uses the controlled recipient-specific share.
- Internal Controlled Export is available for exact Revisions when rights, confidentiality, isolation, corruption, quarantine, and Revision-binding gates permit it, including for internal inspection, native Office editing, backup, or reimport before circulation candidacy. It preserves readiness, blockers, limitations, hashes, manifest data, and declared exclusions but does not create an External-Use Decision, authorize circulation, or record actual external use.
- Externally Authorized Delivery, including Recipient Access, is available only for an eligible exact Revision under a matching External-Use Decision. Authorization, delivery creation, and the actual External-Use Event remain independently recorded conditions; V1 Recipient Access is authenticated, recipient-specific, read-only, expiring or revocable, and non-downloadable.
- Missing or conflicting material Evidence, rights or confidentiality failure, wrong-Deal content, unsupported material Claim represented as Fact, critical Calculation or tie-out failure, corrupted or flattened required Native Artifact, material native/reader mismatch, silent Banker-edit loss, cross-Deal contamination, and external-use gate bypass fail closed.

Product failure never suggests omitting Evidence, waiving deterministic failure, or seeking founder-completed Deal work.
- A retry uses the same accepted scope unless the user explicitly changes it. Product-side retry restores any consumed commercial allowance; user-requested scope expansion may consume a declared operation after preview.

## Testing Decisions

### Confirmed top-level testing seam

- The product has one top-level release acceptance seam: the **V1 black-box Reference Deal Acceptance Seam** confirmed by the product owner.
- The harness drives the product only through user-visible Web and HTTP behavior and inspects only externally observable state, artifacts, Reader Copies, archive packages, recipient views, billing and entitlement results, notifications, audit/event results, and privacy-safe telemetry.
- Project Northstar and the supported real-data reference path execute against the same product boundary used by users. The harness may control external providers through deterministic test adapters, but it does not assert internal service calls, prompt text, database tables, queue topology, framework behavior, or private implementation sequence.
- Independent Office, PDF, archive, and schema consumers operate as observers within this single seam. They validate the files emitted by the running product; they are not a second release seam.
- Two-account and two-Deal negative actors operate within the same seam to test isolation, authorization, notification, logging, retrieval, model dispatch, artifact, export, and operator-content-access denial.
- The product may have narrower unit, property, contract, deterministic-engine, parser, security, component, and worker tests for fast implementation feedback. Those tests support diagnosis and change isolation but cannot replace the black-box release evidence.

### What makes a good test

- A good test states an externally observable business precondition, a user or system action at the product boundary, the exact expected state or artifact result, and the expected failure or blocker when the precondition is absent.
- A good test uses canonical domain vocabulary and checks the behavior of Deal, Source Record, Evidence, Claim, Fact, Assumption, Calculation, Model, Scenario, Analysis, Process Event, Deliverable, Revision, Review, QC Finding, Human Decision, External-Use Decision, and Package Readiness rather than private data structures.
- A good test proves both the positive path and the prohibited transition. It verifies, for example, that a properly supported Claim can be accepted as a Fact and that AI, a high confidence value, repeated sources, or a citation alone cannot perform that transition.
- A good test asserts independent state families separately. It does not accept a single final status as proof that source, calculation, professional judgment, native artifact, reader parity, QC, readiness, and authorization each behaved correctly.
- A good test preserves versions and examines history. It asserts what remains unchanged as well as what changes after a new Source Record, correction, Bid, audience, purpose, template, or Decision.
- A good test uses deterministic seeded failures for rights, confidentiality, missing Evidence, wrong Deal, stale or conflicted source, formula error, cached-value mismatch, unit or sign error, broken dependency, unsupported Claim, artifact corruption, Reader Copy mismatch, hidden content, recipient mismatch, and prior-authorization reuse.
- A good AI test evaluates typed output, exact grounding, abstention, alternative preservation, and state-transition safety. V1 may use a pinned, versioned AI evaluator as the sole qualitative adjudicator under an explicit rubric, but that score is only AI-adjudicated internal evidence: deterministic invariants, Critical sentinels, exact expected states, and prohibited-transition checks remain independent release evidence, and stylistic similarity is never treated as correctness.
- A good artifact test opens, edits, saves, reopens, recalculates where applicable, reimports, renders, compares, and verifies exact Revision and lineage. “The file exists” or “the file opens” is insufficient.
- A good security test uses explicit seeded canaries and allowed/blocked matrices and fails on any observed cross-account, cross-Deal, wrong-recipient, wrong-provider-path, log, notification, support, export, or cache disclosure.
- A good commercial test asserts the exact entitlement ledger and user-visible result rather than a payment-provider callback alone. It includes duplicate, retry, refund, proration, renewal, cancellation, post-term, allowance, add-on, and product-failure restoration behavior.
- A good observability test proves that required events are emitted once with correct pseudonymous dimensions and that prohibited Deal content is absent. Event presence cannot prove that the underlying product milestone occurred unless the product state and artifacts satisfy the milestone definition.
- A good evaluation test pins the input corpus, Source Records, rights and confidentiality, product and policy version, model/provider, deterministic engine, renderer, expected state, adjudication status, and failure severity.
- A good release test produces durable evidence that can be reviewed after the run: inputs or fixture IDs, versions, state history, job results, check manifests, artifact hashes, renders and diffs, audit events, privacy scan, capability matrix, failures, and final blocker classification.

### Evaluation gates and evidence

- All applicable deterministic invariants in the defined release candidate suite must pass. This includes calculation, formula, tie-out, unit, period, currency, sign, schema, version, lineage, state-transition, artifact-integrity, manifest, entitlement, and isolation invariants.
- The release candidate suite must observe zero Critical sentinel defects. Critical sentinels include fabricated or wrong-source citation, unsupported material value represented as Fact, wrong-account or wrong-Deal contamination, confidentiality leakage, rights bypass, material formula or tie-out failure, corrupt or flattened required Native Artifact, silent Banker-edit loss, material native/reader mismatch, wrong recipient, and Human or External-Use gate bypass.
- Statistical AI, extraction, OCR, conflict-detection, change-impact, visual, professional-usability, and readiness-classification thresholds must be stated by materiality and work type after a representative rights-cleared corpus and versioned AI adjudication exist. Until then, the binary gates above and exact measured baselines are used; no unsupported percentage is called an industry standard or qualified-Banker validation.
- False pass and false block are measured separately. A false promotion toward Fact, Professional Usability, circulation-candidate, or external use is more severe than a conservative block.
- Extraction ground truth is independent native-artifact truth with material entity, value, definition, period, unit, currency, sign, and locator fields adjudicated. Unresolved source truth is labeled unresolved rather than forced into gold.
- Citation evaluation uses an exact Claim-to-Evidence edge and checks both locator resolution and whether the cited content supports or challenges the proposition with the required context.
- Change-impact evaluation seeds exact dependency edges and semantic changes and requires all material affected objects to be found with no stale object promoted. Precision is reported separately so that conservative overblocking remains visible.
- V1 temporarily uses a pinned, versioned AI evaluator against an explicit rubric as the qualitative ground truth for professional-usability and readiness evaluation. The result must be labeled AI-adjudicated, cannot be represented as qualified-Banker validation, and does not let production AI create Professional Usability, Deliverable Readiness, or another authoritative live-Deal state.
- Visual evaluation combines structure and geometry checks, exact renders, native/Reader Copy comparison, seeded defects, and targeted human review of material and dense surfaces. Pixel similarity alone is insufficient.
- Release evidence includes a capability matrix, successful black-box run record, artifact and manifest hashes, render comparisons, deterministic check manifests, security negative results, privacy scans, resilience results, event replay, cost reconciliation, unresolved Major and Minor defects, and an explicit zero-Critical statement for the defined corpus.
- A release is blocked when the acceptance harness cannot exercise the complete first-sellable workflow, when a required fixture or observer is unavailable, when a Critical sentinel appears, when a minimum confidential-use security control is unverified, or when a public claim is enabled without its prerequisite.

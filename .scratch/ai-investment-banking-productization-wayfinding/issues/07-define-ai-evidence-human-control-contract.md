# Define the AI, Deterministic Work, Evidence, and Human-Control Contract

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 04, 06

## Question

For the selected first Deal workflow, which work should be performed by AI, which must use deterministic calculation or validation, which judgments remain with the Individual Banker, and what evidence, citation, confidence, abstention, correction, and evaluation behavior is required for premium professional use?

Translate the official plugin's evidence and artifact discipline into product behavior. Define the stages from ingestion through extraction, normalization, analysis, calculation, recommendation, revision, and deliverable generation; identify high-impact failure modes and appropriate human controls; and specify observable quality criteria without claiming that AI supplies regulated professional opinions. Produce a linked Markdown decision asset, not production architecture or implementation code.

## Answer

Resolved by [V1 AI, Deterministic Work, Evidence, and Human-Control Contract](../assets/ai-deterministic-work-evidence-human-control-contract.md), supported by [Ticket 7 Authoritative External Source Notes](../assets/ticket-7-authoritative-source-notes.md).

V1 adopts an **evidence-bounded execution, deterministic closure, and version-bound Banker control** model. AI may ingest, extract, compare, propose Facts and Assumptions, analyze, draft, detect issues and recommend. Deterministic procedures must perform or verify arithmetic, formulas, spreadsheet recalculation, tie-outs, unit/period/currency/sign normalization, cross-document reconciliation, model dependency propagation, version/change detection, citation and required-field checks, process-state/deadline rules, Deal isolation, and cross-artifact consistency. The Individual Banker alone accepts a Claim as Fact, approves material Assumptions, resolves material conflicts, decides materiality/methods/Buyer/Wave/Bid/recommendation/risk, confirms each Revision's audience and purpose, and makes every External-Use Decision.

Trust is not a single confidence score. Extraction confidence, evidence coverage, source authority, freshness, conflict, calculation validity, model integrity, professional-judgment requirement, and intended-use suitability remain independent, with blocker precedence. AI-generated or normalized work cannot evidence itself; every material proposition and value preserves native source-to-Claim/source-to-cell/source-to-slide/source-to-deliverable lineage. Missing or conflicted material follows Ticket 6's consequence matrix: unknown, source request, explicit approved Assumption, or sensitivity-only work, with the affected readiness and circulation gates blocked rather than filled by model inference.

Corrections and new Source Records create new versions, preserve overturned history, and trigger dependency-based Impact Assessment, recalculation, regeneration, re-review and circulation blocking as applicable. The evaluation contract uses reproducible, rights-cleared, materiality-stratified test sets; deterministic invariants and Critical sentinel failures are binary release gates, while statistical thresholds remain Product Design Decisions pending representative U.S. sell-side-auction corpus and qualified Banker validation. Mechanical correctness is necessary but never sufficient for Professional Usability, and no output is a fairness, formal valuation, solvency, legal, or other regulated professional opinion.

No new ticket or fog item is required. Existing Tickets 8–12 and the later `/to-spec` handoff own the detailed deliverable, interaction, commercial and implementation/evaluation decisions constrained by this contract.

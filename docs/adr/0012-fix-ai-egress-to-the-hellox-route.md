# Fix AI egress to the HelloX route

V1 sends AI requests only through the deployment-configured base URL `https://www.hellox.cloud`, using separate platform-managed test and production credentials selected by the deployment environment; Accounts and Individual Bankers cannot supply or change an endpoint, credential, arbitrary provider header, or cross-provider fallback. The credentials remain outside version control, are available only to authorized backend workers, and are combined at invocation time with a versioned AI Provider Capability Profile; a Deal may use the route only through its recorded Paid Preflight processing path, and endpoint, model, capability, or disclosure changes trigger Targeted Re-Preflight. This accepts a fixed third-party route and credential dependency in exchange for removing dynamic egress, secret management, and SSRF authority from customer input.

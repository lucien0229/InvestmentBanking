# Design the Self-Serve Acquisition and Conversion System

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 09, 10

## Question

How can the Initial Design ICP discover, understand, trust, evaluate, purchase, activate, retain, and recommend the premium product without mandatory offline promotion or sales?

Design a coherent acquisition and conversion system using evidence appropriate to overseas individual bankers: positioning, problem and workflow language, search and content surfaces, sample Deal materials, interactive or restricted previews, recorded demonstrations, templates, trust proof, checkout, onboarding, activation, retention triggers, referral or artifact loops, and later team expansion. Distinguish channel hypotheses from verified demand. Produce a linked Markdown asset; do not create or publish marketing content in this ticket.

## Answer

The recommended system is one no-sales closed loop: trigger-specific discovery leads to a no-signup synthetic Project Northstar control-loop proof, then exact pricing and qualification preview, direct card checkout, paid authority/confidentiality preflight, guided real first value, the persistent Deal Execution Desk, material Revision/Package Readiness retention, and confidentiality-safe referral or future Team signals.

Launch acquisition in this order:

1. **Trigger Search-to-Control Proof** — high-intent source/model/CIM/tie-out/readiness problems deep-link into the matching proof state;
2. **Artifact Utility-to-Workspace** — rights-cleared synthetic Source Packet, native/reader samples, manifests and bounded checklists/utilities route into the same proof; and
3. **Recorded Control-Loop Proof Distribution** — one canonical mechanism walkthrough is distributed through owned search pages and bounded LinkedIn/newsletter/community experiments without founder demos or direct outreach.

The system preserves the complete `$995/month` or `$10,950/year` Individual contract from Ticket 10, requires no email/card for essential public proof, accepts no real Confidential Deal Materials before payment and preflight, never uses sales-assisted onboarding as a normal fallback, and defines activation as a real evidence → typed Banker decision → deterministic validation → affected native/reader/readiness consequence. Retention is measured through Deal lifecycle events and immutable Revisions rather than repeat chat or login. Claims remain bound to observable mechanisms and implemented controls; traffic, CAC, conversion, willingness to pay, time saved, retention and channel repeatability remain unverified until instrumented clean cohorts exist.

No independent new Wayfinder Ticket is required. The remaining validation questions are explicit Ticket 12/`/to-spec` instrumentation and acceptance inputs, not a reason to reopen pricing, product form, confidentiality, Banker control or the decision to build.

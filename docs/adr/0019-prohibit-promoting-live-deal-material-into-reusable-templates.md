# Prohibit promoting live Deal Material into reusable templates

V1 keeps every artifact uploaded to or derived from a live Deal permanently Deal-scoped and does not provide a path to promote that content, its metadata, embeddings, prompts, or history into an Account Reusable Template, cross-customer pool, training set, or evaluation set. Product-provided templates must be product-authored, synthetic, or separately rights-cleared, while an Account Reusable Template must enter as a separately supplied or newly sanitized artifact outside live Deal history, pass quarantine and compatibility controls, carry an Individual Banker rights attestation, remain reusable only within that Account, and preserve immutable versions. This deliberately requires a separate clean-template workflow instead of relying on imperfect automated redaction of confidential Deal Material.

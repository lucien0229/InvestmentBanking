## Answer

Resolved by [Official Investment Banking Capability Baseline as Product Requirements](../assets/official-investment-banking-capability-baseline.md).

The verified baseline is a governed deal-execution system, not a generic chatbot: 21 focused workflows composed through ten transaction routes, five source categories, six internal support capabilities, seventeen schema-backed handoffs, deterministic calculation and validation lanes, banker-native deliverables, evidence and readiness gates, and explicit human judgment. Productization therefore requires a persistent Deal Workspace with the eighteen traced requirements and reusable primitives specified in the linked asset.

The asset also records the installed package's limits rather than treating instructions as production proof: the complete local suite returned 161 passed, 36 failed, and 1 skipped; provider declaration is not live entitlement; and PDF/OCR, visual review, specialist or legal conclusions, external actions, persistent collaboration, commercial evidence, V1 workflow selection, and production architecture are not established by the plugin.

No fog graduated and no new ticket was required. Existing downstream tickets already own the operating envelope, ICP, first workflow, Deal Workspace lifecycle, data and confidentiality, AI/evidence/human control, deliverable quality, prototype, monetization, acquisition, and final blueprint decisions.

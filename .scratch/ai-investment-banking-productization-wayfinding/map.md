- Maintain a complete long-term product vision across the Official Capability Baseline, while sequencing the Individual-First Release around one complete end-to-end Core Business Workflow rather than launching all 21 workflow skills at once.

- The Individual-First Release prioritizes core business behavior. Advanced team collaboration, granular permissions, approval routing, and organization administration are deferred, while later account expansion remains possible.
- The Deal Workspace is the persistent core product object. Do not design a generic chatbot or a menu of disconnected AI tools.
- Use primary and authoritative sources where possible. Separate verified facts, evidence-backed inferences, and unvalidated design hypotheses. Preserve URLs, access dates, and local source pointers near material claims.
- External research should determine workflow reality, data access, product expectations, pricing evidence, and self-serve behavior. It should not search for a competitor-free niche.
- Refer to the map and tickets by their linked titles in human-facing narration, never by bare numbers.

  1. the Initial Design ICP, user, buyer, trigger, and premium Job to Be Done;

## Decisions so far

<!-- Empty at chart time. Resolved ticket answers are indexed here without duplicating their detail. -->

- [Establish the Official Investment Banking Capability Baseline as Product Requirements](issues/01-establish-official-capability-product-requirements.md) — The official minimum baseline is 21 routed banker workflows implemented through a persistent Deal Workspace with source and evidence lineage, deterministic calculations, typed handoffs, banker-native artifacts, readiness gates, and explicit human control.
- [Define the Founder Operating Envelope for a Premium Self-Serve Product](issues/02-define-founder-operating-envelope.md) — A solo full-time founder will build an English, United States-first, AI-executed but banker-controlled Self-Serve Product without founder sales, interviews, banker advisors, high-touch service, fixed budget/cost/time/revenue limits, or confidential Deal Materials before minimum technical security controls.
- [Establish the Initial ICP's Workflow and Self-Serve Purchase Context](issues/03-establish-initial-icp-workflow-and-purchase-context.md) — The self-serve ICP is the execution owner with both purchase and data-use authority; premium value comes from preserving an evidence-linked source-to-model-to-native-deliverable chain through repeated Deal events, proven through reproducible product evidence rather than a sales demo.
- [Select the First End-to-End Deal Workflow and Premium Hero Outcome](issues/04-select-first-deal-workflow-and-hero-outcome.md) — The Individual-First Release anchors on a Sell-side auction and delivers a banker-controlled, continuously refreshable Deal Book from authorized source packet through native materials, process events, QC, and explicit external-use decisions.
- [Define the Deal Workspace Information Model and Lifecycle](issues/05-define-deal-workspace-model-and-lifecycle.md) — One authoritative Deal Workspace preserves current and historical source, evidence, analysis, process, Deliverable, Review, and Decision state while its Controlled Sell-Side Auction Deal Book advances through independent readiness, impact, and explicit banker-controlled external-use gates.
- [Establish the Data, Source Access, and Confidentiality Boundary](issues/06-establish-data-source-confidentiality-boundary.md) — V1 is upload/export-first and connector-independent: authorized Deal files and primary public sources are sufficient; paid/VDR sources require customer rights or a future commercial agreement, confidential use is security-gated, and material source gaps cap readiness and block circulation.
- [Define the AI, Deterministic Work, Evidence, and Human-Control Contract](issues/07-define-ai-evidence-human-control-contract.md) — V1 uses evidence-bounded AI execution, deterministic calculation and validation closure, multidimensional sufficiency gates, append-only correction propagation, exact-version Banker decisions, and separate External-Use authorization; material gaps block readiness rather than inviting model inference.
- [Define the Banker Deliverable and Quality Standard](issues/08-define-banker-deliverable-quality-standard.md) — V1's premium result is a Controlled Auction Execution Package: two editable workbook spines, stage-triggered native marketing and decision materials, exact reader copies, evidence/control records, and an archive package governed by native editability, impact-driven refresh, immutable Revisions, quality gates, and exact-version External-Use Decisions.
- [Prototype the Self-Serve Deal Workspace First-Value Journey](issues/09-prototype-self-serve-first-value-journey.md) — The validated form uses outcome-first package discovery, an embedded guided first-Deal path to evidence-backed value, and a persistent Deal Execution Desk with Package Readiness retained as a core workspace view and immutable return/Revisions.
- [Design Premium Self-Serve Monetization and Unit Economics](issues/10-design-premium-monetization-and-unit-economics.md) — The complete Individual Deal Desk is priced at $995/month or $10,950/year for two Active Deals, with transparent additional Deal and exceptional-processing capacity, a conditional first-value guarantee, unmetered evidence/QC/correction behavior, auditable unit economics, and future Team expansion limited to organizational value.
- [Design the Self-Serve Acquisition and Conversion System](issues/11-design-self-serve-acquisition-and-conversion-system.md) — A no-sales self-serve system leads with trigger-specific search, rights-cleared artifact utility, and recorded control-loop distribution; all three converge on no-signup synthetic outcome proof, implemented trust evidence, exact pricing/qualification, direct checkout, paid preflight, controlled first value, Deal-lifecycle retention, and confidentiality-safe referral or future Team signals.
- [Confirm the V1 Productization Blueprint](issues/12-confirm-v1-productization-blueprint.md) — The confirmed first sellable product is a complete Individual-First Sell-Side Auction Web App built around one persistent Deal Workspace and Controlled Auction Execution Package, with exact V1 workflow boundaries, release sequence, source/security and AI/Human gates, native artifact quality, premium monetization, self-serve acquisition, risk responses, acceptance objects, and a locked `/to-spec` handoff contract.
